# DigiCard
![Screenshot 2024-08-18 205941](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/70d14799-aa15-4f74-83fe-652c7149d71f)

On Digicard, you can share your profile with others and display all of your social network connections.
### Features

- **User Registration**: Create a unique URL by choosing a username.
- **Email Verification**: Register using your email and password, and verify your email via a confirmation link.
- **Profile Setup**: Add your name, bio, and upload a profile and banner photo.
- **Contact Methods**: Add multiple contact methods such as phone number, WhatsApp number, and links to your social media accounts.
- **Profile Sharing**: Share your unique URL so others can contact you through your preferred methods.
- **Edit Profile**: Update or edit your contact information and profile settings anytime.
